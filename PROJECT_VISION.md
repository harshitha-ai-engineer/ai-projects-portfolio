# 🌟 Project Vision

To build a practical enterprise AI platform that combines Generative AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Agentic AI to solve real-world business challenges.

- Build intelligent AI-powered applications.
- Implement Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
- Develop autonomous AI agents.
- Enable intelligent document processing.
- Design scalable and modular AI architecture.
- Follow enterprise-level development practices.

## Long-Term Goal

To develop a production-ready Enterprise AI Platform that demonstrates real-world AI engineering capabilities and solves practical business problems.
